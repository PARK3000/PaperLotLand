## Recommendation: Migrate to New Site

### Why We Recommend Migration

1. **Performance**: The new site is significantly faster, which directly impacts:
   - Google rankings (page speed is a ranking factor)
   - User experience and bounce rates
   - Mobile conversion rates

2. **Security**: WordPress sites require constant security updates and are frequent targets for hackers. The new site eliminates these risks.

3. **Cost Savings**: Lower hosting costs and no need for premium WordPress plugins or themes.

4. **SEO Preservation**: We've maintained all URLs exactly as they are, so there will be zero impact on your existing rankings.

5. **Modern Foundation**: Built on technology that major companies use (Netflix, Uber, Nike), ensuring long-term support and improvements.

6. **Competitive Edge**: Your competitors are still on WordPress. A faster, more modern site gives you an advantage.
